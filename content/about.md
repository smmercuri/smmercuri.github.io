# Formalising mathematics in Lean

I am a Chapman-Schmidt Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at Imperial College London, 
where my research focuses on number theory and formal proof verification in [Lean](https://lean-lang.org). 

I have been involved in:
- a [formal verification](https://github.com/smmercuri/zeta) of the Riemann hypothesis up to height $10^8$.
- the project to [formalise Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean](https://imperialcollegelondon.github.io/FLT/).
- contributing to and maintaining [Formal Conjectures](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures), 
  a repository of open research-level problems in mathematics formalised in Lean.
- the development of [AlphaProof](https://deepmind.google/blog/ai-solves-imo-problems-at-silver-medal-level/), 
   which reached silver-medal standard at the 2024 International Mathematical Olympiad.
