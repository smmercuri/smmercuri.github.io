The images on this site are phase plots of complex functions from number theory, such as the [Riemann zeta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_zeta_function). 
Each point of the complex plane is coloured by the argument of the function's value, forgetting its magnitude; zeros and poles appear where all the colours meet.

I made them with Arb, a C library for arbitrary-precision ball arithmetic that is now part of [FLINT](https://flintlib.org/), the Fast Library for Number Theory.
